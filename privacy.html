Privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026

The Behind Every Buy publishing tool ("the app") is a private application used only by the owner of the Behind Every Buy YouTube channel and Instagram account to publish their own videos.

What the app accesses

  • With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the app uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and reads the status of those videos (scopes: youtube.upload, youtube.readonly).
  • With the owner's permission through Meta, the app publishes Reels to the owner's own Instagram professional account.
  • The app does not access, collect or store data about any other person.

How data is stored and used

  • Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository.
  • Video titles, video IDs and publish times are stored in that private repository to avoid duplicate uploads.
  • Data is used only to publish and schedule the owner's videos. It is never sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and in Instagram's settings under Apps and websites.
